# Backdrops

`default.png` is the community backdrop, a dojo at night (original brand art: moonlit shoji screens, a round
moon window, a paper lantern, a plank floor, CRT scanlines; `skills/badges/gen_backdrop.py`, #235), used for every system that has no backdrop of its own. To give a system its own look, drop your own art here, named
`<system>.png` (the ES-DE system theme name, e.g. `cps2.png`, `neogeo.png`), 1440x1080.

Everything here except `default.png` and this README is git-ignored: your art stays on your
machine and is never committed or redistributed.
